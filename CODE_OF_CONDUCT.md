# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where anyone working on building and energy
data can contribute, whatever their background, employer, experience or first
language. Much of the useful knowledge here comes from people who spend their
days in plant rooms rather than in code review, and that knowledge is welcome
in whatever form it arrives.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about technical problems and civil about people. "This query will
  read the whole table on a backfill" is useful; "did you even test this" is
  not.
- Assume the other person knows something you do not. In this domain they
  usually do.
- Accept that a decision may already have a reason recorded next to it in
  `ROADMAP.md`. Argue with the reason, not with the person who wrote it down.
- Ask before sharing anything from someone else's site: credentials, exports,
  building names and meter serials are not ours to publish.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sustained disruption,
publishing others' private information, and sexualised language or imagery.
Also: knowingly filing an issue that discloses a live vulnerability in
somebody's deployment — use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, discussions, and
anywhere someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the maintainers listed in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); they will respond privately and will not share
your report further than necessary to resolve it. Maintainers who do not
follow this code are subject to it too, and reports about a maintainer can go
to any other maintainer.

Consequences escalate with the behaviour: a private word, a public correction,
a temporary block, a permanent one.
